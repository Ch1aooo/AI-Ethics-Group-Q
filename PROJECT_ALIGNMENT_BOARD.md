## 1 Product purpose 产品目标

- Help a student or researcher compare a planned research idea with relevant existing work.
- Use similarity to identify papers, methods, datasets, protocols or equipment that may help the project.
- Use distinctiveness to show how the idea differs from the retrieved work and which differences merit further investigation.
- Support sustainability through opportunities for reuse and efficient use of research resources. The environmental benefit remains a hypothesis to evaluate.
- Allow research descriptions or records to be tagged so that other researchers can discover relevant work, subject to sharing choices and permissions.

## 2 Scope and boundaries 范围与边界

### Core scope from the user direction

- A website, a research-description input and a source-backed results view.
- Two outputs: **Similarity Tree** and **Distinctiveness Tree**.
- A **Knowledge Landscape Circle** to orient users within the selected research landscape.
- Tags supporting discovery and potential reuse.
- Possible research directions integrated into the distinctiveness analysis. They do not require a separate third tree.

### Earlier features outside this core

- Environmental-footprint calculations and the battery-batch assessment flow.
- The old Assess, Check & Learn and Query module structure.
- The three-tree design currently written in Section C.

These remain in the historical records. Any feature brought back into the current product needs a recorded reason and group agreement.

### Claims to qualify in the report and pitch

- The circle is a conceptual visualisation, not a measured boundary of all human knowledge.
- Distinctiveness is relative to the records retrieved. Missing work may change the conclusion.
- A proposed frontier direction is a question to investigate, not a proven research breakthrough.
- Similarity does not itself demonstrate duplication, lack of value or avoidable resource use.
- A possible reuse opportunity is not evidence of measured energy or material savings.
- The current work is a design/mock-up unless an implementation and evaluation are actually documented.

## 3 Assessment dimensions 判断维度

### Similarity 相似性

- Compare topic, research question, method and relevant resources.
- Attach retrieved records and a short explanation to each branch.
- Explain what the researcher may be able to borrow or reuse and what needs checking.
- Keep the source link visible so the user can inspect the original work.

### Distinctiveness 独特性

- Describe differences from the retrieved records, for example in question, application or method.
- Link comparisons to their evidence and make missing information visible.
- Let the researcher inspect and correct misleading comparisons.
- Treat a difference as a basis for discussion with a supervisor rather than proof of novelty.

### Possible frontier directions 前沿探索方向

- Use the distinctive aspects to identify questions for further investigation.
- State that these directions depend on the search coverage and the interpretation of sources.
- Do not present an untested numerical “frontier score” as a scientific measurement.
