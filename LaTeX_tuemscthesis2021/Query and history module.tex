\subsection{Query and history: an illustrative dialogue}
\label{sec:query-dialogue}
This screen sketch follows a researcher through the third module, \emph{I want to Query}. Blue bubbles show researcher input; green bubbles show the proposed tool's response. Case identifiers, counts and measurements are demonstration data, not records from an existing TU/e database.

\newtcolorbox{querysystem}{enhanced,colback=green!5,colframe=green!45!black,

\newenvironment{userturn}{\begin{flushright}\begin{queryuser}}{\end{queryuser}\end{flushright}}

\subsubsection*{1. My History: view and manage past assessments}
\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher}\par
I want to see the sustainability self-assessments I ran earlier this semester.
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
Your private history contains \textbf{two past assessments}:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Case \#2026-A:} Electric Vehicle Battery Fabrication --- completed; shared with the proposed restricted case archive.
  \item \textbf{Case \#2026-B:} Machine Learning Model Training --- draft; private.
\end{itemize}
Would you like to open a report or change a sharing choice?
\end{querysystem}

\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher action}\par
\emph{Change Case \#2026-A from ``Shared'' to ``Private''.}
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
Case \#2026-A is now private and no longer appears in shared search results. Your own history still contains it. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled; the interface should explain this limit before the change is confirmed.
\end{querysystem}

\subsubsection*{2. Database Search: find cases, practices and guidance}
\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher}\par
I'm planning a project on battery testing cycles. Search for similar research and relevant sustainability guidance.
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
In this demonstration, the proposed restricted archive returns \textbf{12 reviewed cases} and \textbf{two guidance documents} related to battery laboratory testing.
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Closest case:} Case \#TUe-BATT-88 --- lithium-ion battery cycle testing using grid electricity. The code replaces identifying details in the shared view.
  \item \textbf{Related guidance:} a battery-testing efficiency note. Its source, version and reviewer would be shown here once approved.
\end{itemize}
The tool may retrieve these sources, but an authorised reviewer must approve case summaries and guidance before reuse.
\end{querysystem}

\subsubsection*{3. Filters and Categories: narrow the results}
\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher}\par
That's too many results. Filter by \emph{Electrical Engineering} and \emph{measured electricity use}.
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
\textbf{Active filters:} Electrical Engineering; measured electricity use (kWh).\par
The illustrative results narrow from \textbf{12 to three cases}. Some describe laboratories using local renewable-energy microgrids. Before comparing them with your project, check equipment, test duration, measurement boundary and research purpose.
\end{querysystem}

\subsubsection*{4. Details and Download: inspect evidence}
\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher}\par
Open Case \#TUe-BATT-88. I want to download its data and formulas to help plan my own project.
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
\textbf{Case \#TUe-BATT-88 --- detailed indicators}\par
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Electricity:} 420 kWh, reported as measured by a dedicated laboratory meter.
  \item \textbf{Missing information:} the upstream lithium supplier's carbon footprint is unavailable, so this impact is not estimated.
  \item \textbf{Possible practice:} the case reports a 12\% reduction in estimated grid emissions after shifting test cycles. This figure needs its time-specific emission factors and calculation method before it can guide another project.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Downloads:} the approved equipment-data CSV and a calculation sheet with formulas, units, boundaries and source versions. Only data cleared for sharing can be exported. No aggregate sustainability score is shown.
\end{querysystem}

\pagebreak
\begin{userturn}
\textbf{Researcher}\par
The spreadsheet shows a carbon result, but I cannot find the emission factor or its source. Where did that number come from? I need to cite it in my lab report.
\end{userturn}

\begin{querysystem}
\textbf{Assessment tool}\par
You are right: the export does not document the emission factor. The carbon result should be marked \emph{unverified} until the factor's value, unit, date, location and source are supplied. The calculation sheet can then show the formula \texttt{=B2*C2} alongside those details. I have flagged this case for curator review; I cannot provide a citation that has not been verified.
\end{querysystem}
